Frequently Asked Questions

How can I reach the corporate headquarters from Cairo International Airport?
Our corporate office in Al Golf, Nasr City is located approximately 15-20 minutes away from Cairo International Airport via Oroba Road or the El-Nasr Road corridor, making it easily accessible for international partners.
Who should I contact for regional hotel partnerships or event bookings?
For cross-brand bookings, hotel investments, or event alliances featuring Grand Ocean Sokhna, Stay Inn Pyramids, or Stay Inn Cairo, please select the Corporate Partnership line via the global query box above or contact corporate@grandoceans.com directly.
What are the business operational hours for the Nasr City headquarters?
Our corporate offices operate from Sunday through Thursday, 09:00 AM to 17:00 PM (EEST). Saturday sessions run on an abbreviated scale from 10:00 AM to 14:00 PM, while operations remain closed on Fridays.
